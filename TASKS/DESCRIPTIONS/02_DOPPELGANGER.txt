ENEMY 02: DOPPELGANGER
=======================

Concept
-------
A dark-purple copy of the player that recreates recent movement. Multiple copies form a delayed trail behind the player.

Base behavior
-------------
- Wait 2 seconds before spawning.
- Spawn at the followed entity's position from 2 seconds earlier.
- The first Doppelganger follows the player's position history with a 2-second delay.
- Each later Doppelganger follows the previous Doppelganger's position history with a 2-second delay, forming a chain.
- Maximum active number: 3.
- Use the player's sprite silhouette tinted dark purple.
- Damage the player on contact. The delayed trail should pressure the player to keep moving and weave between the Doppelgangers.

Enemy-buff modifier options
---------------------------
Choose one modifier effect for this enemy:
- Increase its maximum active number by 2, from 3 to 5.
- Reduce the delay between each Doppelganger and the entity it follows from 2 seconds to 1 second.

Implementation and testing notes
--------------------------------
- Record enough position history to replay the required delay for the player and each preceding Doppelganger.
- Test what happens when the followed entity dies, respawns, changes level, or crosses a collapsing platform.
- Decide whether Doppelgangers collide with terrain/platforms or replay the path regardless of terrain.
