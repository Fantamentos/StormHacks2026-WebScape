ENEMY 03: STOPWATCH
===================

Concept
-------
A timed threat that warns the player before dealing damage. The player avoids the hit by briefly stopping all input.

Behavior
--------
- Maximum active number: 1.
- Spawn 7-10 seconds after the level begins, with the delay randomized.
- No additional spawn grace period; begin the enemy's cooldown/countdown immediately when it spawns.
- Show an 8-second countdown above the enemy.
- If the countdown reaches zero before the player has been idle for 0.2 seconds, damage the player.
- If the player gives no control input for 0.2 seconds before the countdown expires, cancel the countdown and reset the randomized cooldown.

Implementation notes
--------------------
- Display the remaining countdown time above the enemy and keep it readable during play.
- Treat movement, jump, dash, and other player controls as input for the idle check.
- Keep the enemy count capped at one.
