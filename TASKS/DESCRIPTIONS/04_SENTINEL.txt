ENEMY 04: SENTINEL
==================

Concept
-------
A mobile ranged enemy that avoids the player and fires small, visible red bullets along straight paths. It does not damage the player on contact.

Spawn and movement
------------------
- Reuse the Dasher's spawn setup: 2-second grace period and a random spawn point in the area around an outermost traversable platform.
- Reuse the Dasher's flight/dash movement rules, including passing through terrain and staying stationary between dashes.
- Choose dash targets that increase distance from the player; use the Dasher-style movement telegraph before repositioning.
- Contact with the Sentinel does not damage the player.

Ranged attack
-------------
- Fire once every 3 seconds.
- Each bullet travels in a straight line at a fixed speed slower than the Dasher's dash speed; gravity and other forces do not bend its path.
- Make bullets small red dots, smaller than collectible dots but still easy to see.
- At each shot, randomly choose one target point: the player's current position or one of six vertices on a hexagon surrounding the player.
- Lock the chosen point when fired; show no path indicator for bullets.
- Remove the bullet when it reaches its target point.

Modifier options
----------------
Choose one buff:
- Fire one additional bullet per shot.
- Increase fixed bullet speed and improve accuracy by reducing the radius of the target hexagon.

Implementation questions
------------------------
- Determine the Sentinel's maximum active count.
- Define how far from the player the Sentinel tries to stay and how it picks a safe dash destination.
